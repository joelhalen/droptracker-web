# Your Clan, Tracked — voice-over read sheet

Deadpan, unhurried, faintly amused. Think "nature documentary narrator who has seen one clan bingo too many".
Leave a beat of silence where a line ends in a joke; the edit holds on the picture there.
Record each numbered line as its own take (file name = the number) so the edit can drop them onto the timeline.
The times below are the current takes in `tooling/vo/promo1/`; a human read can run a little either side.

**01** · take 10.0s

> Somewhere, a clanmate just got a Twisted bow. They're looking for the screenshot key. Your Discord will hear about it in forty minutes. Or never.

**02** · take 2.9s (+0.6s hold after)

> Or it could hear about it in seconds.

**04** · take 17.0s

> The DropTracker is a RuneLite plugin and a Discord bot, working as a pair. Your members just play. The plugin spots the loot, the personal bests, the collection log slots and the pets, takes the screenshot itself, and sends it in.

**05** · take 13.1s

> A moment later it's in your Discord. The item, what it's worth, the kill count, the screenshot, and where they now rank in your clan this month. Nobody typed a thing.

**06** · take 8.0s

> You decide what that message looks like. Every part of the design is yours to change, and it's free.

**07** · take 17.9s

> And you decide what deserves one. Drops under two and a half million stay quiet by default, so nobody's announcing rune scimitars. Personal bests, pets and collection logs can each get their own channel. Deaths are available too, if your clan enjoys that.

**08** · take 18.3s

> Meanwhile the lootboard keeps score: a picture of this month's loot, redrawn on its own and edited in place. And your clan gets its own pages on droptracker.io. Useful for settling arguments. Or starting them.

**09** · take 7.5s

> Membership follows your Wise Old Man group and syncs every hour. New recruits show up. People who left, leave.

**10** · take 12.0s

> And because someone will always try it: big drops are checked against the OSRS Wiki's drop tables. A Twisted bow from a goblin doesn't get recorded, let alone announced.

**11** · take 18.5s

> When you want more, run bingos that score themselves, keep a clan points ledger, or post a Hall of Fame of your fastest kills. Points and the Hall of Fame are on paid plans. The notifications, the lootboard, the designs and your clan's pages are free.

**12** · take 12.6s

> Setup takes a few minutes. Pick your Discord server, invite the bot, paste in your Wise Old Man group, and choose a channel. Then tell your clan to install the DropTracker plugin.

**13** · take 2.6s (+1.2s hold after)

> Screenshots were never the fun part anyway.
