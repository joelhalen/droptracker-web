# How Scoring Works — voice-over read sheet

Deadpan, unhurried, faintly amused. Think "nature documentary narrator who has seen one clan bingo too many".
Leave a beat of silence where a line ends in a joke; the edit holds on the picture there.
Record each numbered line as its own take (file name = the number) so the edit can drop them onto the timeline.

**01** · target 5.2s (+1s hold after)

> Points. The only thing clans love more than drops, and arguing about drops.

**03** · target 11.2s

> Every task is worth some points. When a team finishes it, the team gets those points. Simple. What's less simple is who on the team gets the credit.

**04** · target 15.2s

> Points are split by contribution. If two of you each got half of a five point task, you each get two and a half. So yes, the person who carried the team gets to prove it, with decimals.

**05** · target 15.6s

> Bingo adds bonuses. Finish a row, a column, or a diagonal and your team gets line points on top. Fill the whole board for the blackout bonus. Empty cells are free spaces, already done for everyone, as a treat.

**06** · target 8.8s

> Filter the board by team to see exactly who's close to a line, and who's one Vorkath kill away from a breakdown.

**07** · target 10.4s

> Standings update live, here and in Discord. The Discord leaderboard is one message, edited in place, so your channel doesn't turn into a wall of scoreboards.

**08** · target 11.6s

> Every point has a receipt. The completion history shows who pulled what, for which team, and when. It is very useful for settling arguments, and for starting new ones.

**09** · target 11.2s

> Then there's effort. Hours spent on things that actually move the board, not hours spent five-manning a boss that isn't on it. Busy isn't the same as helpful.

**10** · target 22.0s

> When it ends, you can pay out. The prize pot tracks buy-ins and donations, but it's advertised only. No real gold moves, you still trade it in game. Clan points go out for placement and participation. And by default, the person who signed up and went AFK on the winning team gets nothing. Justice. Finally.

**11** · target 13.6s (+1.5s hold after)
